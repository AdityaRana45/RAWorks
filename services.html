Top Tips to Keep Your Bike in Excellent Condition

  • Regularly check and change engine oil to ensure smooth running.
  • Monitor tire pressure and tread to maintain safety and efficiency.
  • Inspect battery health and clean terminals to avoid electrical issues.
  • Check brakes frequently for wear and tear and replace pads timely.
  • Clean or replace air filters to keep the engine breathing properly.
  • Schedule regular servicing with trusted mechanics to prevent major issues.
  • Keep the chain lubricated and properly adjusted for smooth rides.
  • Tighten nuts and bolts periodically to ensure safety on the road.
  • Avoid harsh acceleration and braking for longer vehicle life.
  • Clean your bike regularly to prevent rust and maintain looks.
